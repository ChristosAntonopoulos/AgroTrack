## Purpose

Working notes for the AgroTrack Privacy Policy. The final, published policy must be reviewed by a lawyer and lives on our public website.

## What the Privacy Policy must cover

- Who we are (legal entity, contact).
- What data we collect.
- Why we collect each piece of data.
- How long we keep it.
- Who we share it with (sub-processors, payment providers).
- Where it is stored (geography).
- The user's rights (access, rectification, erasure, etc.).
- How to contact us about privacy.
- Cookie use on the public site.
- How and when the policy might be updated.

## Tone

- Plain language.
- Short sentences.
- No legalese where we can avoid it.
- "You" and "we", not "the data subject" and "the controller".

## Sections (draft outline)

1. Who we are.
2. The data we collect — with examples.
3. Why we collect it.
4. How long we keep it.
5. Who we share it with.
6. Where it is stored.
7. Your rights.
8. Cookies and tracking.
9. Changes to this policy.
10. Contact us.

## What we will NOT do

- Sell data.
- Use customer data to train external AI models without explicit consent.
- Share data with marketing partners.

## Open Questions

- Do we publish a separate cookie policy or roll it into the Privacy Policy?
- What is our retention policy for closed accounts (30 days? 90 days?)?
