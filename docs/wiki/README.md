## What is AgroTrack

AgroTrack is an agricultural lifecycle management platform for field owners, producers, farmers, agronomists, and the teams that work the land together. It helps them manage fields, follow each crop through its lifecycle, track tasks and costs, collaborate across roles, and keep a clear history of what happened on every field, every season.

We are starting with a focused vertical (olive cultivation) and a small, validated MVP — and expanding from there.

## What this wiki contains

This wiki is organised so a new team member can read it top to bottom and understand the whole business in under an hour. It covers:

- The company, its vision and the team
- The business: problem, customer, market, competitors, pricing, go-to-market
- The product: what the MVP is, what is out of scope, what comes later
- Customer research: interview guides, notes, and what we learn
- Scrum and delivery: how we work, how we plan, how we ship
- Design, marketing, legal and compliance notes
- Meetings and templates we reuse

## How the team should use it

- **Read first, build second.** Before starting work on a feature, find the relevant page here.
- **Update as you learn.** If a page is outdated, fix it. Don't wait for the "owner".
- **One page, one purpose.** If a topic doesn't have a page yet, add one.
- **Use the templates** in `11 Templates` for meeting notes, interviews, feature specs, decisions and risks.
- **Move stale pages** to `/Archive` instead of deleting them, so history is preserved.

## Current phase

We are in the **validation and MVP definition** phase. Concretely, that means:

1. Validating the problem with real customers through interviews (`04 Customer Research`)
2. Defining a focused MVP we can build and ship quickly (`03 Product/MVP Definition`)
3. Setting up Scrum so the team can deliver predictably from Sprint 0 onwards (`06 Scrum and Delivery`)

Heavy technical work and scaling come **after** validation. The technical section here is intentionally light at this stage — it grows as the product grows.
