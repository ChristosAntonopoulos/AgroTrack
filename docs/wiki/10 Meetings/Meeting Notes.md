## Purpose

A running index of meeting notes for AgroTrack. Each meeting gets a section here, using the [Meeting Notes Template](../11%20Templates/Meeting%20Notes%20Template.md).

## Conventions

- One section per meeting, newest at the top.
- Title format: `YYYY-MM-DD — Meeting name`.
- Decisions made go to the [Decision Log](../01%20Company/Decision%20Log.md).
- Action items get an owner and a date.

3. Always capture: attendees, agenda, decisions, action items.
