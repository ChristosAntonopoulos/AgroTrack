This Privacy Policy explains how The Olive Lot (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website at theolivelot.com and our mobile applications for Android and iOS, including the Android app com.theolivelot.app (together, the “Service”).

1. Who we are (data controller)

Data controller: The Olive Lot operators, trading as The Olive Lot.
Country: Greece
Privacy & support: support@theolivelot.com
Web: https://theolivelot.com

When a registered Greek company is formed for The Olive Lot, this section will be updated with the exact legal entity name and registered details. Until then, privacy requests are handled by the The Olive Lot operators at the contacts above. The developer identity for the Google Play listing for The Olive Lot / com.theolivelot.app is the same product identity described here.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email address, password (stored hashed), language and preference settings, and role within an organization or field.
  • Grove and work data — field names, sizes, map locations and coordinates, notes, tasks, observations, harvest, expense and income records, and related timestamps.
  • Photos and media — images you capture or select for field records. On device we may read photo metadata (including location tags) only to help match photos to fields; nothing is uploaded until you confirm.
  • Device location (optional, foreground only) — approximate or precise location only when you explicitly use a feature that needs your current position (for example “Use my location” to place a field). We do not use background location. After a grove’s coordinates are saved, weather and field context use those saved coordinates, not continuous phone tracking.
  • Contacts (optional, single picker) — if you choose one contact via the system contact picker to save a person (for example a pruner or mill). We do not request broad address-book access and we do not import your full phone book.
  • Notifications — device push tokens if you enable notifications.
  • Technical and security data — IP address, device/app version, and basic login or request logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Messages you send us — feedback, support requests, and contact forms.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising networks or data brokers. We do not run third-party advertising or behavioural tracking SDKs in the apps. We do not collect payment card numbers in the app. The Service is not directed at children under 16.

4. How we share data

We share data only as needed to run the Service:

  • People you invite — members of a field or workspace see the grove data and photos that their permissions allow.
  • Our hosting infrastructure — application servers and databases that store The Olive Lot data for theolivelot.com / api.theolivelot.com (including MongoDB software running as part of our deployment, and local application file storage for uploads).
  • Email delivery — transactional email via SMTP configuration we operate (password reset, invites, support routing to support@theolivelot.com).
  • Push delivery — Expo’s push service (and, on Android, the underlying device push channel) to deliver notifications you enable. Push tokens are stored with your account until you disable notifications or delete the account.
  • Geospatial and weather providers — we send field coordinates or map areas from our servers to providers such as Open-Meteo, Earth Search / satellite imagery sources, SoilGrids, Terrascope land-cover, and NASA FIRMS (country-level fire data) so we can show weather and field context.
  • Map tiles and geocoding on your device — basemap tiles (for example OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap, Esri imagery) and Nominatim search/reverse geocode when you search or place a location.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

We do not share your data with advertising networks or sell it to data brokers. We do not use crash-analytics or advertising SDKs that profile you for ads.

5. Where data is stored and international transfers

Application data is stored on the infrastructure we use to operate theolivelot.com and api.theolivelot.com. Our primary production intent is to keep application databases and uploaded files in the EU/EEA. Some processors we call for product features (for example weather, satellite discovery, map tiles, or push delivery) may process limited technical or location-related data on servers outside the EEA. Where that happens, we rely on the provider’s applicable transfer mechanisms (such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses) where required.

6. Retention

  • We keep account and grove data while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, login is closed immediately; name and email are removed from the account record; push tokens, in-app notifications, and contacts you saved are deleted; you are removed from shared fields; fields you own are archived immediately and become inaccessible through the product.
  • Retention criterion for permanent deletion of archived grove residuals (including photos and operational records tied to fields you owned): within 30 days of the account’s deletion timestamp.
  • Encrypted backups may retain residual copies for up to 90 days after deletion, then expire under backup rotation.
  • Limited security or legal records may be retained longer where the law requires it.
  • While a field remains active, generated satellite overlays follow an operational retention window (currently up to 400 days); they follow the field through archival and post-deletion cleanup.

Account deletion instructions (in-app and by email): https://theolivelot.com/delete-account/

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Data portability (export a copy of your data)
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and related personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (location, camera, photos, notifications) in your device settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA)

In the product: Settings includes data export and Delete account. You can also email support@theolivelot.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Device permissions

  • Location (when in use only) — requested only when you use a feature that needs your current location, such as positioning a field on the map. Not used in the background. Weather for a grove uses the grove’s saved coordinates.
  • Camera and photos — evidence and Photo Hub; upload only after you confirm.
  • Contacts — system contact picker for a single person you choose; no broad address-book permission.
  • Notifications — optional operational alerts.

You can deny or revoke these permissions. Some features will then be limited.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses cookies or local storage that are necessary to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as language. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or advertising tracking pixels.

10. Children

The Service is intended for adults managing agricultural work. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
