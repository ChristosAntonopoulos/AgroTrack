Delete your The Olive Lot account

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it. This page is the public account-deletion resource for The Olive Lot (Android package com.theolivelot.app and theolivelot.com).

In the app (recommended)

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm with your password and email. You will be signed out immediately.

Outside the app

Email support@theolivelot.com from the email address on your account.

Subject line example: Delete The Olive Lot account. Include the account email you use to sign in. We will verify ownership and complete deletion.

What is deleted

  • Your login and profile (name, email, password) — closed immediately
  • Device push tokens
  • In-app notifications for your user
  • Contacts you saved in The Olive Lot
  • Your membership on shared fields
  • Fields you own — archived immediately so they are no longer usable in the product; associated photos, tasks, notes, and other personally associated grove records are permanently removed under our post-deletion cleanup criterion (within 30 days of deletion)

What may remain briefly, and why

  • Encrypted backups may retain residual copies for up to 90 days after deletion, then expire under backup rotation.
  • Limited security or legal records may be kept where the law requires it.
  • Grove data that another collaborator still needs on a shared field remains available to that collaborator after your membership is removed.
